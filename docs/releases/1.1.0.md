## ULLeds 1.1.0

### Added

* **Track insights:** Open the disclosure in Spotify Sync to see the genre
  matched from the track title and artist in Apple's iTunes catalog, with
  Windows media genres as a fallback, plus playback state and live audio
  readings.

### Improved

* **Spotify Sync follows the music more deliberately.** It adapts its response
  to track-level audio, detects bass, midrange, and treble attacks, and adds
  restrained accents for strong energy surges. Genre hints adjust the response
  for a wider range of styles when a catalog or media-metadata match is
  available; tracks without a genre match still respond to their audio. Genre
  labels are looked up from track metadata rather than guessed from audio
  bands.
* The Spotify artwork palette remains the color source, with bounded movement
  and response rather than random spectrum-driven recoloring.
* The Lighting preview follows the frame actually sent to the LEDs, including
  brightness, power, effects, and channel calibration. Animated effects also
  continue in the preview when the LEDs are disconnected.
* Palette drift colors can now be edited, reordered by dragging, and removed on
  PC and phone while keeping at least two colors.
* Temporary override controls on the phone can be tucked away behind the
  app's existing animated disclosure.
* Desktop and phone controls are more consistent: palette handles and remove
  icons match, sync buttons use text alone, and interface icons share a common
  stroke weight.
* Spotify's audio privacy explanation lives in the README instead of being
  repeated beneath the Sync control.
* Every tab now fills the window with equal side gutters, and titles stay
  fixed on a matching background while content scrolls. In Lighting, the
  preview fills the window and the controls panel fits its content, growing
  smoothly as options open and scrolling inside itself only when the window is
  too short. Scrollbars everywhere end before rounded corners.
* The phone remote mirrors the desktop app's layout and visual system, including
  dialogs, typography, colors, spacing, surfaces, and dark and light appearance,
  while using its own `Lighting`, `Scenes`, and `Settings` tabs. Screen Sync and
  Spotify Sync sit inside `Lighting`; Sync preferences and phone pairing live
  under `Settings`. Lighting controls include per-LED selection, channel
  balance, and temporary overrides; `Scenes` supports saving, applying, and
  deleting setups with confirmation.

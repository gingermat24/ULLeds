# Future ideas

## Fortnite Sync

Deferred: explore whether ULLeds can read Fortnite's on-screen Visualize Sound
Effects indicators and map their colors, direction, and intensity to the LEDs.
Do not read game memory or inject into the game. Before any implementation,
research current anti-cheat rules and account risk; on-screen capture alone is
not a guarantee that the approach is allowed or ban-safe.

Color cues to investigate: white for footsteps, vehicles, animals, gliders,
and general movement; gold for chests and loot; orange/red for gunshots and
explosions; green for healing and revives; and blue for Reboot Vans. Indicator
brightness/fade represents proximity; indicator position around the screen
represents direction as the camera turns.
